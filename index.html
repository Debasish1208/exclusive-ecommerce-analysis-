The Brief

Why this project exists

Exclusive's transaction ledger arrived the way most real retail data does: inconsistent category casing, four different date formats, currency symbols glued to prices, and duplicate rows from a flaky export job. The task was to turn that into a trustworthy source for three things stakeholders actually asked for — where the revenue is coming from, whether customers come back, and which fulfilment issues are worth fixing first.

Step Two — SQL Analysis

Where the revenue actually moves

Monthly Revenue — Jan 2024 to Dec 2025

Revenue holds a steady band through most of the year and lifts every October as festive-season buying kicks in — visible in both 2024 and 2025 — before cooling into December. That seasonal spike is the clearest single planning signal in the dataset for inventory and marketing spend.

-- Monthly revenue performance
SELECT Order_Month,
  ROUND(SUM(Revenue),2) AS Revenue,
  COUNT(DISTINCT Order_ID) AS Orders
FROM transactions
WHERE Order_Status != 'Cancelled'
GROUP BY Order_Month
ORDER BY Order_Month;
Step Two — SQL Analysis

Category mix: volume vs. value

Revenue by Category (₹)

Payment Method Share

  • Electronics drives 60% of revenue from just 12% of orders — average order value of ₹46,514 versus ₹1,100–10,500 everywhere else. A single high-ticket category is carrying the top line.
  • Card and cash-on-delivery are neck and neck at 25.4% and 24.9% of orders respectively, with UPI, debit, and net banking splitting the rest almost evenly — no single digital rail has won yet.
  • 25–34 year olds are the core customer, generating ₹3.73 Cr of revenue across 4,020 orders — more than double any other age band.

Step Four — Automated Reporting

Excel + Power BI, built to update itself

The Excel workbook uses SUMIFS/COUNTIFS pivot-style formulas against the raw data sheet rather than static numbers, so refreshing next month's transactions recalculates every KPI, chart, and category breakdown automatically — this is the same structure that cut manual reporting time by roughly 40% in production use. The Power BI layer sits on top of the same cleaned table for interactive drill-down by month, category, and city.
